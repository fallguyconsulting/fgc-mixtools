# Targets

Use these numbers when the person has no reference song, or to sanity-check a reference. A reference song the person likes beats any number in this file.

## While mixing

| Measure | Target | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sample peak | Below about -3 dBFS | Leaves room for the final loudness step. |
| True peak | Below 0 dBTP | Above 0 dBTP the mix can distort on playback. |
| Clipping | None | Clipping is audible distortion. |
| Stereo correlation | Mostly between 0 and +1 | Below 0 the song loses parts when played in mono, such as on a phone or a smart speaker. |

Mix loudness does not matter yet. A good mix is often 6–12 dB quieter than a finished release.

## For release

Streaming services turn loud songs down to a set level. A song mastered louder than that level gets turned down and gains nothing. A song mastered quieter may get turned up, or may play quieter than other songs.

| Service | Normal playback level |
| --- | --- |
| Spotify | -14 LUFS |
| YouTube | about -14 LUFS |
| Amazon Music | about -14 LUFS |
| Tidal | about -14 LUFS |
| Apple Music (Sound Check) | -16 LUFS |

Keep true peak at or below -1 dBTP for release. Services convert files to lossy formats, and the conversion can push peaks above 0.

Rough integrated loudness of commercial releases, by style:

| Style | Typical range |
| --- | --- |
| EDM, hip-hop, loud rock and metal | -9 to -6 LUFS |
| Pop | -10 to -7 LUFS |
| Singer-songwriter, folk, jazz | -16 to -11 LUFS |
| Classical, film score | -23 to -16 LUFS |

These ranges describe what people release, not what anyone should aim for. If the person asks how loud their song should be, -14 to -10 LUFS with -1 dBTP true peak suits most streaming releases. Going louder costs punch and dynamics.

## Loudness range (LRA)

| Style | Typical LRA |
| --- | --- |
| EDM, pop, hip-hop | 3–7 LU |
| Rock, singer-songwriter | 5–10 LU |
| Jazz, classical, film | 10–20 LU |

A loudness range far below the reference means the mix is over-compressed. Far above means the quiet parts may get lost in a car or on a phone.
